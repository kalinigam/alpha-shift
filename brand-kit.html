01  —  Logo & wordmark

The wordmark

Alpha Shift is written as one confident word-pair in Geist, with a single orange full stop — the small, deliberate mark where a thought lands. The stop is the brand’s quiet signature: the moment something settles.

Alpha Shift.
Alpha Shift.on paper
Alpha Shift.on ink
Alpha Shift.on orange

Keep the orange stop; it is the one flourish.

Give it clear space of at least the cap-height of “A” on every side.

Set it in Geist Semibold, tight tracking.

Don’t recolour the letters — they stay ink, white, or reversed.

Don’t stretch, outline, or add a second accent to the mark.

Don’t drop the stop or swap it for a period in the text colour.

04  —  Voice

How it sounds

Human, intelligent, thoughtful — a little playful. It asks before it tells. It never oversells. Copy is Chetan’s own words; the design bends to the copy, never the reverse.

Human, not corporate

Plain language, short sentences, room to breathe. Speaks to a person with something on their mind.

Questions over claims

Opens space instead of closing a sale. “Something on your mind?” not “Transform your leadership.”

Quietly confident

No hype words, no exclamation. The calm of someone who has done the thinking.

You don’t need to have it figured out. Bring the question. We’ll start there.
